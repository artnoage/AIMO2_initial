# Product Context: Mathematical Problem-Solving Framework

**Why this project exists:**

The project exists to address the need for a systematic and comprehensive approach to evaluating and improving the mathematical problem-solving abilities of language models. While language models have shown impressive capabilities in various domains, their performance on complex mathematical reasoning often lags behind. This framework provides the necessary tools and infrastructure to benchmark these capabilities rigorously and to train models specifically for mathematical tasks using reinforcement learning.

**Problems it solves:**

*   **Lack of Standardized Evaluation:** Provides a suite of diverse benchmarks to evaluate different facets of mathematical problem-solving, moving beyond simple answer correctness to assess reasoning steps, code generation, error identification, and architectural planning.
*   **Inefficient Model Training:** Offers a GRPO framework that enables targeted fine-tuning of models on mathematical tasks, utilizing techniques like dynamic rewards and parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA) for improved efficiency and performance.
*   **Data Quality and Management:** Addresses the challenge of obtaining and managing high-quality mathematical problem datasets by providing tools for filtering, processing, and standardizing data for training and evaluation.
*   **Reproducibility and Analysis:** Facilitates reproducible research and detailed analysis of model performance and training dynamics through comprehensive logging, statistics tracking, and integration with tools like Wandb.

**How it should work:**

The framework should allow users to:

1.  **Prepare Datasets:** Use the auxiliary tools to process raw mathematical problem datasets, ensuring they meet quality standards and are formatted correctly for benchmarking and training.
2.  **Run Benchmarks:** Execute various benchmark scripts to evaluate the performance of language models on different mathematical tasks. Configure benchmarks with options for model selection, dataset filtering, execution settings, and output formats.
3.  **Train Models:** Utilize the GRPO training scripts to fine-tune language models on prepared datasets. Configure training with options for learning rates, batch sizes, reward functions, and logging.
4.  **Analyze Results:** Examine the detailed statistics, reports, and datasets generated by benchmarks and training runs to understand model strengths and weaknesses, track progress, and identify areas for improvement.
5.  **Export and Deploy Models:** Export trained models in optimized formats for deployment and further evaluation.

**User experience goals:**

*   **Ease of Use:** Provide clear documentation, command-line interfaces, and sensible defaults to make the framework accessible to researchers and developers.
*   **Flexibility:** Allow users to configure various aspects of benchmarking and training to tailor experiments to their specific needs.
*   **Reproducibility:** Ensure that experiments can be easily reproduced through clear configuration options and detailed logging.
*   **Insightful Analysis:** Provide comprehensive statistics and reporting to give users deep insights into model performance and training dynamics.
*   **Efficiency:** Leverage techniques like parallel processing, caching, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning to make benchmarking and training efficient.
